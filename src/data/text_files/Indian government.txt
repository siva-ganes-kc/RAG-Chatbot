The Government of India is the central government responsible for governing the country. 
    India follows a parliamentary democratic system and is a federal union of states and union territories. 
    The government has three main organs: the Legislature, Executive, and Judiciary. 
    The Parliament consists of the Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha. 
    The President is the constitutional head of the country, while the Prime Minister leads the Council of Ministers and the central executive. 
    The Supreme Court of India is the highest court and plays an important role in interpreting the Constitution and protecting citizens' rights.
