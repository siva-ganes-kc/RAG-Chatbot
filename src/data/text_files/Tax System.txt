India has a multi-level tax system managed by the Central Government, State Governments, and local authorities. 
    Taxes are broadly divided into direct taxes and indirect taxes. Direct taxes, such as income tax and corporate tax, are generally paid directly by individuals or businesses to the government. 
    Indirect taxes are collected on goods and services, with Goods and Services Tax (GST) being the major indirect tax in India. 
    GST is a destination-based tax administered jointly by the Centre and the States.
    Other taxes and duties may include customs duties, excise duties on certain products, and state-level taxes and fees.
